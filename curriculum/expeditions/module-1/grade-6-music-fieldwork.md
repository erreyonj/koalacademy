## Before — music class

### Music question

**What story is still being told here, and what did people have to do to keep it alive?**

In *The Lightning Thief*, old Greek stories keep showing up in Percy’s world. On this walk,
look for a story Madison still tells in stone, metal, or letters.

**Watch for**

- a name, a Latin word, or an inscription
- a figure from a myth (a god, a hero, a monster, an animal)
- something old that people have clearly repaired, labeled, or protected

## After — next music class

We will hear how some legends stay alive because people keep performing them: Robert
Johnson, Prince, and rappers who work with “no pen.” Then you will try to rap or sing on a
prepared loop without writing anything down.

Use **one detail from During** (a name, a carving, a repair, or your sketch). You may not
answer this only from the listening.

How is a legend kept alive — by stone, by a story people retell, or by performing it without
a script? Use your walk note.
