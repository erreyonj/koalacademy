## Before — music class

### Music question

**What do people keep so a story is not lost, and whose history is in that object?**

*A Long Walk to Water* is about people whose stories almost disappeared. Places and objects
keep some stories; others only survive if somebody saves them.

**Watch for**

- a sign that explains what this place protects
- an object, sample, or tool kept on purpose
- whose name or community is attached to it
