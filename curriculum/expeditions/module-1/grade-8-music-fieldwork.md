## Before — music class

### Music question

**Whose version of this story is on display, and what would change if someone else told it?**

*Summer of the Mariposas* retells old folklore for new readers. Public stories work the same
way: someone chose whose version you see.

**Watch for**

- who is named on the plaque, mural, or monument
- who is missing
- words that sound like one group’s telling (hero, first, founded, claimed)
