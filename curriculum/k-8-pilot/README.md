# K-8 Pilot — One City Schools

This folder is the working home for a K-8 general music program built for One City Schools in
Madison, WI. It adapts [Koalacademy](../KOALACADEMY.md) — originally written with roughly an
8th-grade floor — into a full K-8 trajectory.

Grades 6-8 follow the existing Koalacademy course closely; their syllabi here map to lesson
codes in [KOALACADEMY.md](../KOALACADEMY.md) rather than duplicating content. Most of the new
work in this folder targets K-5, which is unplugged-first: games, movement, listening, and
classroom routines rather than devices.

## Status of this pass

This is a documentation-first pass. The goal is to capture every game, routine, teaching
decision, and framework in durable docs, and to stand up syllabus scaffolds with the right
shape. Year-long pacing, day-to-day plans, and standards alignment get filled in on the next
pass, once meeting cadence per grade is confirmed.

## Relationship to the rest of the repository

- [curriculum/KOALACADEMY.md](../KOALACADEMY.md) remains the source of truth for 6-8 lesson
  content. Nothing in this folder duplicates it.
- [curriculum/VANGUARD SONGS.md](../VANGUARD%20SONGS.md) is superseded by
  [vanguard-songs/framework.md](vanguard-songs/framework.md), which is the formatted and
  extended version. The original is kept for reference.
- [units/](../../units/) folders continue to hold 6-8 materials and activities.

## K-5 strand codes

K-5 lessons and games are tagged with strand codes, the K-5 counterpart to Koalacademy's
component codes (`BMT`, `D4`, ...). Definitions and upward mapping live in
[scope-and-sequence.md](scope-and-sequence.md).

`BEAT` `PITCH` `TIMBRE` `FORM` `NOTATE` `LISTEN` `CREATE`
