# Classroom Setup — One City

> **Status: snapshot.** Live notes from a basement walkthrough. This is a working
> operations list for the music classroom — inventory, layout, and what still needs to be
> acquired — not a syllabus. Photos and video were planned as follow-up context and are
> not in the repo yet. Treat counts as approximate until those exist.

Source: One City basement walkthrough voice note. Goal of this file is a classroom-setup
task list and materials list.

## Context

Recording studios in the basement did not look ready to record. Rooms visited had no
easily visible recording equipment: no studio mics (dynamic or condenser), no mixing
board, no obvious recording chain. Keyboards were visible; cables are assumed but not
confirmed.

This classroom is the one being configured for the K-8 pilot. Audio is the priority for
the space, not a full classroom-stuff overhaul.

## Room layout

Working plan:

- Two long lines of tables down the middle, with an aisle to walk through
- Students face each other and can see the staff board and the TV
- Tables can be pushed into the middle when the room needs to rearrange
- Piano / keyboard in the room
- Plenty of wall space for posters or art — content undecided (music-store posters vs.
  artist / art in general)
