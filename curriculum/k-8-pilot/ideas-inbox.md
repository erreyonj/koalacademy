# Ideas Inbox

Parking lot for raw ideas from chats, observations, and found resources. No formatting rules:
dump it here, sort it later. When an idea graduates, move it to its real home
([playbook/games.md](playbook/games.md) for games,
[resources/booklist.md](resources/booklist.md) for books, syllabi for structure) and delete
it from this list.

## How to use

- One bullet per idea, date optional
- Half-formed is fine — that's the point of the inbox
- Review the inbox when planning each unit/quarter
