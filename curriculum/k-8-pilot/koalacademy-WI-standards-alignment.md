# Koalacademy — Wisconsin DPI Music Standards Alignment

Alignment of the Koalacademy curriculum and the One City K-8 pilot against the Wisconsin
Standards for Music, **General Music (MG)** strand.

> **Status: first full pass.** Every MG performance indicator in the source document is mapped
> to Koalacademy codes and given a coverage flag. Flags reflect what is *written* today, not
> what is planned for the pilot year. This file is the source of truth for the alignment chart
> published at `/syllabi/wi-dpi-standards-alignment`.

## Purpose and sources

The Wisconsin standards excerpt in
[assets/Wisconsin Music Standards.pdf](../assets/Wisconsin%20Music%20Standards.pdf) covers the
General Music content area across four standards — **CREATE (MG1)**, **PERFORM (MG2)**,
**RESPOND (MG3)**, and **CONNECT (MG4)** — organized into four grade bands: K-2 (`e`), 3-5
(`i`), 6-8 (`m`), and 9-12 (`h`). Seventy performance indicators total.

General Music is the correct strand for this program. Wisconsin defines it as formal courses
exploring music in a non-performance setting, explicitly including music technology,
composition, and improvisation — which is what Koalacademy is. The Performance Music (MP)
strand governs bands, choirs, and orchestras; it is not part of the provided excerpt and not
part of this program.

## Overall alignment

| Band | Indicators | `CORE` | `DEV` | `GAP` | Judgment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K-2 (e) | 17 | 7 | 6 | 4 | Directionally aligned; response and listening are strong, creation and performance roles are thin |
| 3-5 (i) | 17 | 7 | 6 | 4 | Same shape as K-2, with stronger notation and vocabulary coverage |
| 6-8 (m) | 18 | 14 | 4 | 0 | Strongest band — every indicator is addressed by written lessons |
| 9-12 (h) | 18 | 15 | 3 | 0 | Content depth is there; no dedicated high-school syllabus exists yet |
| **Total** | **70** | **43** | **19** | **8** | |

Three findings drive everything below.

**Grades 6-8 already satisfy the 6-8 band.** All eighteen 6-8 indicators are addressed by
written lessons, and fourteen are covered outright. Koalacademy's spine — compose in Koala,
analyze records, document rights, present sources, perform live, reflect — maps almost
one-to-one onto CREATE, RESPOND, and CONNECT, and onto PERFORM as Wisconsin scopes it for
General Music.

**K-5 is aligned in intent and unbuilt in practice.** The six documented games and the Vanguard
framework carry real coverage, especially for RESPOND. Everything else rests on one-line strand
emphases in the grade syllabi, with deliverables, pacing, and per-strand mastery rows still
TBD in [scope-and-sequence.md](scope-and-sequence.md). That is why K-2 and 3-5 each carry six
`DEV` cells.

**The eight `GAP` cells cluster into three themes**, all in K-5: audience and performer roles,
concert etiquette, and music's relationship to history, culture, and other disciplines.

**Notes.** Composition in Koala is the strongest single alignment in the document: `AW.3` and
`AW.4` require a complete original arrangement built from documented sources, which is
`MG1.Cr.9.m`, `Cr.11.m`, and `Cr.12.m` in one deliverable. The recurring soft spot across all
four bands is **improvisation**. Rhythmic and textural improvisation exists (`D4.4` note

student to improvise a melody or a harmonic variation over a progression — the explicit demand
of `MG1.Cr.6.i`, `Cr.10.m`, and `Cr.14.h`.

**Notes.** PERFORM is where the General Music reading of the standard matters most. Wisconsin's
"sing or play an instrument" language is satisfied in a music technology course by pad
performance and live sampling, which `AEP.5`, `PERFORMANCE.2`, and `PERFORMANCE.3` cover in
detail — including rehearsal, recovery from mistakes, and device readiness. What is genuinely
thin is **singing on pitch and acoustic technique**: echo singing appears in the K-2 and 3-5
strand lines but no repertoire or vocal-technique material exists, which is why `MG2.P.11.m`
and `P.16.h` sit at `DEV`. The two `GAP` cells are the K-5 **performer and audience roles**
indicators; grades 6-8 handle the same idea well in `PERFORMANCE.4`.

**Notes.** In 6-8 and up, CONNECT is a natural fit that the course reaches almost by accident:
`US.1` traces sampling from Jamaican dub through the 1988 MPC, `SRP.2` and `SRP.5` connect music
to law and business, `SONGFORM.5` compares beat flips to classical movements, and `AEP.4` places
Koala in a technology landscape. `MG4.Cn.12.m` sits at `DEV` rather than `CORE` because the
roles covered — producer, artist, publisher, engineer — are drawn almost entirely from American
popular music, and the indicator asks for **world cultures**. The four K-5 `GAP` cells are the
largest single hole in the alignment: nothing in K-5 currently addresses history, culture, or
musicians' roles.

## Strengths

1. **The 6-8 project spine covers three standards at once.** The `DYS` → `AW` → `AEP` →
   `PERFORMANCE` arc produces a composition (MG1), a live performance with rehearsal and
   critique (MG2), documented analysis and reflection (MG3), and cultural and historical context
   (MG4) from a single body of student work. Standards evidence falls out of the existing
   deliverables without adding assignments.
2. **Technology-based creation is a direct hit.** `MG1.Cr.11.m`, `Cr.12.m`, `Cr.15.h`, and
   `Cr.16.h` all name technology and alternative notation explicitly. Koala sequencing, chopping,
   pad mapping, and step editing satisfy these more cleanly than a traditional general music
   course would.
3. **Analysis and terminology are unusually deep for middle school.** Two full analysis passes
   over a student-chosen playlist (`DYS.3`, `DYS.4`) plus five case studies (`US.2`-`US.5`,
   `SONGFORM.3`, `SONGFORM.4`) exceed what `MG3.R.9.m` and `R.11.m` ask for and reach into the
   9-12 indicators.
4. **Rights and ethics content has no standards equivalent — and that is fine.** `SRP.1`-`SRP.5`
   is not required by any MG indicator, but it strengthens `MG4.Cn.9.m` (music and other
   disciplines) and is a defensible enrichment claim in its own right.
5. **The Vanguard Songs framework is the K-5 alignment engine.** One anchor recording revisited
   through whichever strand is active covers most of RESPOND and half of CONNECT in K-5 with no
   new material.

## Areas for further alignment

Ordered by standards impact per unit of work.

### 1. Close the four K-5 CONNECT gaps — `MG4.Cn.1.e` `Cn.4.e` `Cn.5.i` `Cn.8.i`

The cheapest fix in the document. Each grade already carries a Vanguard Song; add two documented
lenses to the K-5 extension in [vanguard-songs/framework.md](vanguard-songs/framework.md): *who
made this and what was their job* (`Cn.4.e`, `Cn.8.i`) and *when and where does this come from*
(`Cn.1.e`, `Cn.5.i`). Selecting Vanguard repertoire across cultures and eras converts these four
`GAP` cells to `CORE` without adding a lesson.

### 2. Add performer and audience roles to K-5 — `MG2.P.4.e` `P.9.i`, `MG3.R.4.e` `R.8.i`

Four `GAP` cells across two standards, all solved by one addition: a short recurring "audience
job" routine attached to K-5 share-outs, plus concert etiquette taught before any performance.
Belongs in [playbook/routines-and-attention.md](playbook/routines-and-attention.md), with the
end-of-year share-outs named in the K-2 syllabi as the performance occasion.

### 3. Broaden etiquette beyond the classroom — `MG3.R.12.m` `R.17.h`

The indicators ask for etiquette in a **variety** of settings. `PERFORMANCE.4` covers the
classroom. Resolving the open grade-8 question about a public showcase would close this, and
`AEP.5` already produces a performance-ready set that could travel.

### 4. Name improvisation as its own practice — `MG1.Cr.2.e` `Cr.6.i` `Cr.10.m` `Cr.14.h`

Four `DEV` cells, one per band, and the only indicator row that is weak at every level. Options
that fit the existing course: a documented call-and-response improvisation game in
[playbook/games.md](playbook/games.md) for K-5, and an improvisation pass in `AEP.1` where
students play a melodic figure live over their own loop rather than only performing effects.

### 5. Decide the singing and technique stance — `MG2.P.11.m` `P.16.h`

Echo singing is named in the K-2 and 3-5 strand lines but no repertoire or vocal-technique
material exists, and 6-8 performance is entirely pad-based. Either commit to a small amount of
documented singing and instrument technique, or state explicitly in the master syllabus that
this program reads "sing or play" as pad and sampler performance. Both are defensible; the
ambiguity is not.

### 6. Convert the K-5 `DEV` cells by finishing scope and sequence

Twelve of the nineteen `DEV` cells are K-5 and share one cause: the per-strand progression rows
and grade deliverables in [scope-and-sequence.md](scope-and-sequence.md) are still TBD. Filling
those tables — end-of-band targets for `BEAT`, `PITCH`, `TIMBRE`, `FORM`, `NOTATE`, `LISTEN`,
and `CREATE` — moves most K-2 and 3-5 creation and performance cells to `CORE` without new
content, because the intent is already documented.

### 7. Broaden cultural range in 6-8 CONNECT — `MG4.Cn.12.m`

`US.1` already reaches Jamaica; adding one non-Western or diasporic sampling tradition to `US`
or to the Vanguard 6-8 repertoire would satisfy the world-cultures language directly.

### 8. Package a 9-12 offering

The 9-12 column is content-complete because Koalacademy was written with roughly an 8th-grade
floor and documented for middle and high school. What is missing is packaging: no high-school
syllabus, no depth differentiation from grade 8, and no statement of which indicators a
semester-versus-year offering would carry.

## Out of scope

- **Performance Music (MP) strand.** Not part of the provided excerpt and not part of this
  program. If One City adds a performance ensemble, that strand needs its own alignment pass,
  including the four additional performance indicators Wisconsin attaches to grade bands 3-5,
  6-8, and 9-12.
- **National Core Arts Standards.** Wisconsin's standards are derived from NCAS (2014), so this
  mapping is a strong starting point for the NCAS sections still marked TBD in the syllabi, but
  the anchor-standard numbering differs and needs its own pass.
- **Grade-level assessment evidence.** This document maps curriculum to indicators. Which
  artifact proves which indicator for a given student, and how that feeds One City report card
  categories, is open in [resources/written-work.md](resources/written-work.md).
