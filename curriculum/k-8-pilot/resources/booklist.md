# Booklist

A growing list of books, workbooks, and reproducible resources supporting the pilot —
especially the corrective written work described in [written-work.md](written-work.md).

> **Every entry below is a starting candidate, not an endorsement.** Titles were seeded from
> general knowledge of standard classroom-music publishers (Alfred, Hal Leonard, Theory Time,
> etc.) and are **unverified** — confirm each title, edition, grade fit, and reproducible
> license in hand before buying or copying. Update the Status column as items are vetted.

Status values: `candidate` (unvetted), `vetted` (reviewed in hand), `in use`, `rejected`.

## Vetting checklist

Before an item moves from `candidate` to `vetted`:

1. Reproducible/copy license actually covers classroom copying
2. Grade fit confirmed against a real class, not the cover
3. Pages map to specific strands or lesson codes (note them in the Notes column)
4. Cost vs. piecemeal alternatives (TPT, free printables) considered
