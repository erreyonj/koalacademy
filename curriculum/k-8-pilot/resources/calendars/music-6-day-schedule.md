# Music 6-Day Schedule

Posted classroom schedule for **MUSIC — Erreyon Elders**. Source: **2026–27 Master Schedule** MUSIC tab.

This is the working schedule for the pilot. Period lengths and which section sits
in which window come from here. Cycle-day numbers come from the
[Eighth Grade Curriculum Calendar](Eighth%20Grade%20Curriculum%20Calendar.md).

Scholars Academy Music and 7th/8th-grade expedition blocks are on this sheet.
They are not core syllabus classes unless a later pass says otherwise.

## How to read this

One City runs a **Day 1–6** rotation. The first day of school is **Wednesday
9/2/2026 = Day 1**. The cycle advances only on instructional days: holidays and
PD skip a weekday but **do not skip the cycle** (Tuesday 9/8 is Day 4 after
Labor Day). Last day of school is Friday 6/12/2026 = Day 2.

On a regular week with no skipped days, weekday and cycle day line up:

| Weekday | Typical cycle day |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Day 1 |
| Tuesday | Day 2 |
| Wednesday | Day 3 |
| Thursday | Day 4 |
| Friday | Day 5 |

**Day 6 rule.** When the cycle is Day 6, keep **that weekday's printed K–5 and
expedition blocks**. Put **6th Grade Blue** in the last period (2:20–3:20)
instead of that weekday's usual 6–8 section.

Example: Thursday 9/10/2026 is Day 6. Run Thursday's morning and afternoon
(3 Gold, 2 Blue, planning) and end with 6 Blue rather than 8 Blue.

Homerooms are **Blue** and **Gold**. Cadence is section-specific, not "twice a
week for the whole grade."
