# High Quality Student Work

How this room meets EL Education’s High Quality Work dimension when much of the product is
sound: a performance, a Koala project, a keyboard take. Paper and notebooks still follow
[written-work.md](written-work.md). This note is the wall, the working files, and the one
public bounce.

## What the dimension asks

High Quality Work is three things together: the task is **complex**, the piece shows
**craftsmanship through revision**, and someone **outside the class** is the audience.

A documentation wall in another room hangs drawings and drafts. In this room the product is
often heard, not hung. The wall holds the part a visitor can **see**, and points at the part
they can **hear**.

**Show me your greatness** is the student-facing name for that wall and for the one share in
Closing Crew ([routines-and-protocols.md](../playbook/routines-and-protocols.md#show-me-your-greatness)).
A piece earns the wall when the student can say **what they revised**. Everything else is
practice.

## Three homes for the work

| Home | What lives there | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Working files | Daily Koala projects, in-progress pads | The student, in **their** folder (Clever) |
| Practice evidence | Keyboard studies and graded reps, labeled `Name-date-skill` | Same student folder; notebook holds the notation |
| Public piece | Finished bounce chosen for the wall | The student’s own Untitled bounce; teacher chooses which QRs go up |

Working files and practice takes use the procedure in
[device-privilege-and-file-management.md](device-privilege-and-file-management.md): Clever
sign-in, a **per-student** folder the classmate cannot write, sign-out at the lab exit.

Practice takes stay in that same folder. A few, spread across the quarter, are what you
would play to show progression. Most of them never get a QR.

The public piece is a finished bounce from **that student’s own** Untitled account. You
choose which links go on the wall. Families and the art room are the audience.

### The wall card

For a piece that earns the wall:

- Album art (often made in art class)
- Student name
- One sentence on **what they changed**
- QR to that student’s bounce

Put only finished, revised pieces on the wall. Daily pads, Do Nows, and scale reps stay in
the working record.

### K–5

K–5 still get a wall. Their greatness is the visible work already in the gradebook: rhythm
pages, listening sentences, lyric drafts, and a photo or short recording of a performance
with the same revision card. The **ready-5th** iPad rotation uses the same Clever sign-in and
per-student folder when those students touch devices.

## Station recording (keyboard into Koala)

Student keyboards have no MIDI out. Everyday Koala work is pads and the in-app Keyboard view
(`KSN.2`) — see [teaching-notes.md](../playbook/teaching-notes.md#midi-keyboards).

Apple’s USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter is a **headphone output**. It does not take line in from a
keyboard. A splitter on that cable splits what you hear; it does not create an input.

When a keyboard take needs to live inside Koala, use **one station** for the room:

- A class-compliant interface with a real instrument input and its own headphone jack (an
  iRig-class box, or any USB-C interface with those jacks). One chain, not one per iPad.
- The student plugs the keyboard into the interface, hears themselves in the interface
  headphones, and records onto a pad or into **their** folder as a `Name-date-skill` take.
- A **Show me your greatness** live take can be the iPad mic into Koala. That documents the
  room performance and needs no adapter.

The wall does not depend on the interface. Add the box to
[classroom-setup.md](../classroom-setup.md) when this year’s labs need station recording.
