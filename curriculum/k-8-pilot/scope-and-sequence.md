## K-5 strand codes

K-5 content is organized by strand rather than by unit. Each strand feeds directly into one
or more 6-8 Koalacademy components, so the K-8 vertical alignment is legible at a glance.

| Strand | Covers | Feeds into (6-8) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BEAT` | Steady beat, meter, rhythm, rests, subdivision | `BMT.4`, `BMT.5`, `D4` |
| `PITCH` | High/low, melody, scales, major vs. minor mood | `BMT.1`, `BMT.2`, `BMT.3` |
| `TIMBRE` | Sound sources, sound quality, describing sound | `D4.1`, `US` |
| `FORM` | Repetition, contrast, sections, song shapes | `SONGFORM` |
| `NOTATE` | Symbols, the staff, reading and writing rhythm | `BMT.4` |
| `LISTEN` | Intentional listening, response, discussion | `DYS`, Vanguard Songs |
| `CREATE` | Composing, improvising, performing, sharing | `AW`, `PERFORMANCE` |

Games in [playbook/games.md](playbook/games.md) are tagged with these codes: Beat Freeze and
Silent Ball are `BEAT`, Timbre Scavenger is `TIMBRE`, Form Detective is `FORM`.

## The theory front-loading problem

The existing course stacks nearly all foundational theory into Unit 1: `BMT.1` (notes,
scales, intervals, key signatures), `BMT.4` (the staff, notation, key signatures again, 4/4
counting), and `BMT.5` (3/4, 6/8, drum sounds). That works for a motivated 8th-grade-and-up
audience; it does not work as the opening month of a general music year.

**Pilot rule:** these concepts are spaced across the full school year and introduced when a
lesson or project needs them. A class demonstrating prior knowledge or fast progression can
accelerate through the spiral — the sequence is fixed, the speed is not.
