## Course description

First graders build on kindergarten play: they keep the beat without the teacher counting,
echo short rhythms, describe sounds with a growing timbre vocabulary, and start turning on
A/B section changes without cues. Written work remains coloring and tracing music symbols.

## Strand focus

| Strand | Emphasis this year |
| --- | --- |
| `BEAT` | Primary — steady beat unaided; freeze on rests without a spoken count |
| `PITCH` | Melodic direction (up/down) with hand movement; echo singing grows |
| `TIMBRE` | Two or three descriptor words used correctly |
| `FORM` | A/B turns without teacher cueing |
| `NOTATE` | Tracing quarter notes and rests; symbol recognition |
| `LISTEN` | One Vanguard Song; "what did you notice" as a spoken routine |
| `CREATE` | Choosing sounds/movements to match a feeling |

## Games rotation

Beat Freeze (one bar, count fades out), Timbre Scavenger (teams, justify the find), Form
Detective (uncued). See [playbook/games.md](../playbook/games.md) K-1 and 2-3 variations.
