## Course description

Third graders start playing with knowledge, not just movement: Balloon War Trivia enters as
review, listening responses become sentences, and reading simple rhythms in 4/4 becomes
expected rather than introduced. An optional second Vanguard Song deepens the listening year.

## Strand focus

| Strand | Emphasis this year |
| --- | --- |
| `BEAT` | Reading and clapping simple 4/4 rhythms; downbeat automatic |
| `PITCH` | Major vs. minor mood by ear; scale as a ladder concept |
| `TIMBRE` | Two-property descriptions; classifying classroom instruments |
| `FORM` | Predicting the next section; naming A/B/C aloud |
| `NOTATE` | Fill-in-the-missing-beat pages as correctives; symbol names solid |
| `LISTEN` | One to two Vanguard Songs; sentence-frame journal lines begin |
| `CREATE` | Compose and notate (iconically) a two-bar pattern |

## Games rotation

leaders), Form Detective (prediction mode), Timbre Scavenger (two-property). See
