## Course description

The bridge year. Fifth graders consolidate everything the strands built — internal 4/4
counting, subdivision, staff reading, full-song form, timbre vocabulary — so that 6th grade
can walk into the [Koalacademy](../../KOALACADEMY.md) production course without the theory
front-load that used to open it. First exposure to what sampling *is* happens here, by ear,
without devices.

## Strand focus

| Strand | Emphasis this year |
| --- | --- |
| `BEAT` | Internal counting under pressure; subdivision felt and named |
| `PITCH` | Major/minor scales heard and located; steps vs. skips |
| `TIMBRE` | Drum-sound roles by ear (kick, snare, hat) — pre-`D4` listening |
| `FORM` | AABA and verse/chorus mapped on real songs — pre-`SONGFORM` |
| `NOTATE` | Reading and writing one-line rhythms; key signature *recognition* begins |
| `LISTEN` | One to two Vanguard Songs; journal entries with "why" questions |
| `CREATE` | Group composition performed for the class — pre-`PERFORMANCE` habits |

## Games rotation

Full rotation at its hardest settings: Silent Ball (off-beats, two balls), Beat Freeze
(student-set rhythms), Musical Taboo, Balloon War Trivia (steal mode), Form Detective
(eyes-closed AABA). See [playbook/games.md](../playbook/games.md).
