## Course description

Sixth grade enters the Koalacademy production curriculum: music theory in service of making,
sampling history, drum design, song form, sampling rights, and an original project sampled
from songs the students choose. The 6th-grade lens on it all is *introduction to intentional
listening* — emotional recognition and basic structure (see the Vanguard vertical alignment
in [vanguard-songs/framework.md](../vanguard-songs/framework.md)).

## Year mapping

The existing course is 20 weeks; the school year is roughly 36. Extra weeks absorb the
respaced theory spiral ([scope-and-sequence.md](../scope-and-sequence.md)), Vanguard Song
listening, games, and production-day logistics. The 20-week spine runs roughly four weeks
per unit, stretched across the ~36-week year; exact calendar placement is set per section.

Units follow the five-unit structure in [KOALACADEMY.md](../../KOALACADEMY.md); `C1`/`C2`
are the two components within each unit.

| Unit | Component | Codes | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · C1 | Basic Music Theory | `BMT.1`-`BMT.5` | **Not front-loaded** — spiraled per scope-and-sequence |
| 1 · C2 | Koala Sampler Navigation | `KSN.1`-`KSN.5` | Production days (1:1 iPads) |
| 2 · C1 | Understanding Sampling | `US.1`-`US.5` | Listening-based; no devices needed |
| 2 · C2 | Digital Drumming | `D4.1`-`D4.5` | Production days |
| 3 · C1 | Song Forms | `SONGFORM.1`-`SONGFORM.5` | Pairs with Form Detective foundations |
| 3 · C2 | Sampling Rights | `SRP.1`-`SRP.5` | Discussion-based |
| 4 · C1 | Defining Your Sound | `DYS.1`-`DYS.5` | Playlist project |
| 4 · C2 | Artist Appreciation | `AW.1`-`AW.5` | Final project begins; production days |
| 5 | Advanced FX / Performance | `AEP`, `PERFORMANCE` | Production days; live performance |

## Devices

Thirty iPads, 1:1 on production days. Production vs. unplugged is a lesson choice, not a
rental calendar. Student keyboards have no MIDI out; the teacher keyboard does, by
appointment — see [playbook/teaching-notes.md](../playbook/teaching-notes.md).

Three anchors — locked in [vanguard-songs/song-list.md](../vanguard-songs/song-list.md):
