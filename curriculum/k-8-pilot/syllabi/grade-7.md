## Course description

Seventh grade continues the Koalacademy production curriculum with a sharper lens: *deeper
structural awareness, contrast, and stylistic identity* (see the Vanguard vertical alignment
in [vanguard-songs/framework.md](../vanguard-songs/framework.md)). Extended theory (`BMT.2`
modes, `BMT.3` relative/parallel keys) likely lands here rather than in 6th — see
[scope-and-sequence.md](../scope-and-sequence.md).
