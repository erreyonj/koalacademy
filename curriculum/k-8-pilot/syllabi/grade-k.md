## Course description

Music through play. Kindergartners keep a steady beat with their bodies, freeze on silences,
hunt for sounds around the room, echo short songs, and learn that music has parts that repeat
and change. Everything is movement, voice, and games — no written work beyond coloring music
symbols.

## Strand focus

| Strand | Emphasis this year |
| --- | --- |
| `BEAT` | Primary — steady beat in the body, freezing on rests |
| `PITCH` | High/low with movement, echo singing |
| `TIMBRE` | Naming sounds: smooth, scratchy, short, ringing |
| `FORM` | Same vs. different; cued A/B turns |
| `NOTATE` | Symbol exposure via coloring only |
| `LISTEN` | One Vanguard Song; feelings words and beat-keeping |
| `CREATE` | Free movement and vocal exploration |

## Games rotation

Beat Freeze (one bar, teacher counts aloud), Timbre Scavenger (one class-wide target word),
Form Detective (teacher cues changes). See [playbook/games.md](../playbook/games.md) K-1
variations.
