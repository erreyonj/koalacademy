<Notes>

Movement today is Silent Ball on the downbeat. Skip the two rules slides once the class knows it.

<Activate title="Silent Ball">

Students stand spread around the room or in a circle. One soft ball. Steady 4/4 music or live guitar at a moderate tempo.

1. *I do:* Teacher passes the ball to themselves on beat one several times, counting out loud.
2. *We do:* Class counts the beat out loud while two volunteers pass on beat one.
3. *You do:* Silence begins. The class counts inside and passes only on beat one.

The class wins by chaining clean passes on beat one. Count the chain out loud after each round and try to beat it.

Full rules live in the game bank: Silent Ball (Musical).

</Activate>

For K-1: seated circle, roll the ball instead of throwing, and count out loud for the whole first round.

On the Listen slide, play a short stretch. Who stomps early or late shows who is following neighbors instead of feeling beat one.

</Notes>
