  <Section name="Seven letters">
    <Slide copy kicker="Seven letters">

Almost all the music you know is built from just **seven note names**: **A, B, C, D, E, F, G**. After G, we start over at A.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Seven letters">

Put them in order starting on **C** and you get the **C major scale**: C, D, E, F, G, A, B, C. On a keyboard, those are the **white keys**. Lots of songs live here because it is the simplest map.

</Slide>

  <Section name="Bright and heavy">
    <Slide copy kicker="Bright and heavy">

A **major** scale tends to sound **bright**. A **minor** scale tends to sound **heavier**. You can hear the difference before you can name it.
