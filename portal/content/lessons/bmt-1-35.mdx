## Seven letters

Almost all the music you know is built from just **seven note names**: **A, B, C, D, E, F, G**. After G, we start over at A. That is the whole alphabet of music.

Put them in order starting on **C** and you get the **C major scale**: C, D, E, F, G, A, B, C. On a keyboard, those are the **white keys**. Lots of songs live here because it is the simplest map.

## Bright and heavy

A **major** scale tends to sound **bright**. A **minor** scale tends to sound **heavier**. You can hear the difference before you can name it — that was your Do Now.

## What you should be able to do after this

1. Say the seven note names in order.
2. Find C on a keyboard and walk up to the next C.
3. Hear a bright example and a heavier one and say which is which.
