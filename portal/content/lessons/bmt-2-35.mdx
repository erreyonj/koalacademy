## More than bright and heavy

Last time you met **major** (bright) and **minor** (heavier). A lot of music uses another shape: the **blues scale**. It has **fewer notes** and one **spicy color note** that gives it its sound.

Mr. Erreyon will play it on **guitar** first. The keyboard is the picture so you can see it.

## The blues scale

The blues scale in C is: **C, E♭, F, G♭, G, B♭**, then C again. That one note in the middle — the **G♭**, called the **blue note** — is the lean that makes it sound like the blues.
