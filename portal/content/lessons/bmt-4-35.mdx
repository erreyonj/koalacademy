## The staff is the shared page

A **staff** is **five lines and four spaces**. Notes sit higher or lower on those lines to show **pitch**. Time goes left to right, split into **measures** (bars) by vertical lines. If you can name the parts of the page, you can write an idea down and play it back later.

The **clef** is the curly symbol at the start. The **time signature** is the two stacked numbers. A single **note** sits on a line or space. A **bar** is the space between two vertical lines.

## 4/4

A **time signature** tells you how to count. **4/4** means **four beats** in each bar, and the **quarter note** gets one beat. Count **1-2-3-4**, then the bar line, then **1** again.

## What you should be able to do after this

1. Point to the **clef**, **time signature**, a **note**, and a **bar** on the staff.
2. Say what **4/4** means: four beats, quarter note gets one.
3. Keep a steady **1-2-3-4** while a song plays.
