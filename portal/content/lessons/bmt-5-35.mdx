## Past 4/4

`BMT.4-35` locked in **4/4**: four beats per bar, count **1-2-3-4**. Most pop and hip-hop lives there. Not everything does.

Today: **3/4** and **6/8**. The top number tells you how many beats are in a bar. The **feel** is what your Do Now was sniffing at — lilt vs. rush.

## 3/4

**3/4** means **three** beats per bar. Count **1-2-3**, bar line, **1** again. That missing fourth beat gives 3/4 a **lilt** — a gentle rock, like a waltz.

## 6/8

**6/8** has **six** quick pulses in a bar, usually felt in **two groups of three**: **1-2-3 4-5-6**, with a push on **1** and **4**. It often feels more **rushed** than 4/4.

## The four drum sounds

Every beat is built from four sounds:

| Sound | Job |
| --- | --- |
| **Kick** | The low thump. Usually on beat **1**. |
| **Snare** | The snap. In 4/4, often on beats **2** and **4**. |
| **Hi-hat** | The steady tick that keeps time. |
| **Tom** | The roll that fills a gap before a new part. |
