## Music has feelings

Some music feels happy. Some feels sleepy. Some feels stormy or grumpy.

You can hear the feeling even when nobody sings a word.

Mr. E will play some music.

## You make the move

Today you are the one who decides. When you hear the music, make up a move that matches the feeling. There is no wrong move, as long as it matches.

When the music stops, freeze.

That is the whole game.

<Activate title="Mood Moves">

Students stand in their spots — room to move, no walking the room. First-teach: two feelings, then one new one.

1. *I do:* Teacher plays a **happy** clip and invents a big bouncy move, naming it out loud. Then a **sleepy** clip and a slow sway.
2. *We do:* Class copies the teacher's move on one clip, and practices the freeze.
3. *You do:* A **stormy** clip. Each student makes their own move. Teacher shines one move. Class copies it. When the music stops, freeze.

The class wins the round when everyone freezes cleanly, and the moves match the feeling.

Full rules live in the game bank: Mood Moves.

</Activate>
