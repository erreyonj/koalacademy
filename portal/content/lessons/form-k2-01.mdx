## Songs have parts

A song is not one long blob. It has parts.

Sometimes a part comes back the **same**. Sometimes a new part shows up — that is **different**.

Today we call the first kind **A** and the new kind **B**.

## Hear the change. Show it.

When you hear A, face one way. When you hear B, turn the other way. Do not wait to see your friend turn.

That is the whole game.

<Activate title="Form Detective">

Students stand in the middle of the room. Agree on signals before you start: face the windows for **A**, face the door for **B**. Use a song with clear A and B parts. Teacher cues the first few changes.

1. *I do:* Teacher plays both sections, names them A and B, and models the turn on the change.
2. *We do:* Class listens to one full A/B cycle and calls out "change!" together before adding the turn.
3. *You do:* Music runs. Students turn on their own. Watch who turns late — they are copying neighbors.

The class wins when the turn happens together, on the change.

Full rules live in the game bank: Form Detective.

</Activate>
