## What is Koalacademy?

This is music class. We **play**. We **listen**. We **learn**.

Some of you already know songs. Some of you are just starting. That is okay. We learn together.

This year you will:

- Keep a beat with your body
- Hear high and low
- Name sounds you like
- Talk about music you love

The big goal: you feel like **you** can do music.

## What is music?

Music is sound with a plan. A beat. A tune. A feeling.

Here are some names we will hear this year:

**Bruno Mars. Billie Eilish. BLACKPINK. Vanessa Carlton. Missy Elliott.** And more.

Do not worry. You can tell Mr. E a song **you** love. We will try to bring it in.

Two things we have to remember:

1. **We do not all start in the same place.** Some friends already know a lot. Some friends are hearing this for the first time. We help each other.
2. **A lot of people hear the same songs.** Those songs are on the radio, in stores, and on videos. We call that **popular music** — the songs lots of people hear. We will ask: who picked those songs? What about the songs that are not on the radio?

By the end of this year, you will be able to:

| **Make** | Move, sing, and try new sounds. |

| **Connect** | Sit in our circle. Take a turn. Be kind. |

### Our Vanguard Song

A **Vanguard Song** is a song our class keeps all year.

We do not hear it once and forget it. We come back again and again. Every time we know more music, we hear more inside it.

Our song is still being picked. Mr. E will write it on the board when it is ready.

The big question, in fall and again in spring: **What do you hear now that you did not hear before?**

## How we show up

We sit in a **circle**. That is CREW.

In a circle, everyone can see. Everyone can hear. Everyone gets a turn.

In this class:

- Mr. E deserves respect. **You** deserve respect.
- We talk. We listen.
- Mr. E uses **ASL signs** with his hands. We will learn them together.
- We give **shine** — we praise each other a lot.
- We try. We mess up. Then we try again. We do not quit first. We do not complain first. We do not say "I can't" first.
- **Transitions are the key.** Fast, quiet moves from one thing to the next give you the most free time — and they help the whole class win.

### CRISP habits

We practice five habits. Say them with me.

- **Compassion** — I am kind.
- **Risk** — I try new things, even when it is hard.
- **Integrity** — I do the right thing, even when no one is looking.
- **Self-Respect** — I am kind to myself.
- **Persistence** — I do not give up.

<Do title="If you can hear me">

This is how Mr. E gets the room back. We learn it like a game.

1. *I do:* Mr. E says, "If you can hear me, clap once," and claps once. Then twice. Then a little rhythm.
2. *We do:* The class claps with Mr. E. Same number. Same rhythm. Then freeze.
3. *You do:* Mr. E calls. You answer. If the room is not with him, we try again. We do not talk over a noisy room.

Then pick **one** CRISP word. Mr. E says the word. The class says the kid version back:

- Compassion — "I am kind."
- Risk — "I try new things."
- Integrity — "I do the right thing."
- Self-Respect — "I am kind to myself."
- Persistence — "I do not give up."

The class wins the round when the clap comes back together and the words come back together.

</Do>
