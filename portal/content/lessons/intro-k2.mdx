<Notes>

Movement today is If You Can Hear Me. It is how Mr. E gets the room back, so teach it like a game. Skip the two rules slides once the class knows it.

<Activate title="If you can hear me">

1. *I do:* Mr. E says, "If you can hear me, clap once," and claps once. Then twice. Then a little rhythm.
2. *We do:* The class claps with Mr. E. Same number. Same rhythm. Then freeze.
3. *You do:* Mr. E calls. You answer. If the room is not with him, try again. Do not talk over a noisy room.

The class wins the round when the clap comes back together.

</Activate>

**Meet Mr. E.** Also mention: a little piano; loves Pop, Hip-Hop, R&B, Jazz, House, Soul, and Gospel; hopes Swashbuckler gets pictures one day.

**What is music?** Music is sound with a plan: a beat, a tune, a feeling. Names we will hear this year: Bruno Mars, Billie Eilish, BLACKPINK, Vanessa Carlton, Missy Elliott. Students can tell Mr. E a song they love.

Two things to remember:

1. **We do not all start in the same place.** Some friends already know a lot. Some are hearing this for the first time. We help each other.
2. **A lot of people hear the same songs.** That is **popular music** — the songs lots of people hear on the radio, in stores, and on videos. Ask: who picked those songs? What about the songs that are not on the radio?

**Your year.**

| Goal | What that looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Make** | Move, sing, and try new sounds. |
| **Play together** | Keep a steady beat. Freeze when the music rests. |
| **Listen** | Hear high and low. Name a sound — smooth, scratchy, ringing. |
| **Listen** | Come back to our Companion song and notice something new. |
| **Connect** | Sit in our circle. Take a turn. Be kind. |

**Our song.** A **Vanguard Song** is a song the class keeps all year. Ours is a **Companion**: the same song the big kids keep. K-2 keeps it by moving, listening, and naming sounds, without taking it apart. The big question, in fall and again in spring: *What do you hear now that you did not hear before?*

**How we show up.** Mr. E uses **ASL signs**; the class learns them together.

**CRISP.** Full habits: Compassion (I am kind), Risk (I try new things, even when it is hard), Integrity (I do the right thing, even when no one is looking), Self-Respect (I am kind to myself), Persistence (I do not give up). On the last CRISP slide, pick **one** word. Mr. E says it; the class says the kid version back. The class wins when the words come back together.

</Notes>
