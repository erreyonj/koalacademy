  <Section name="A sampler">
    <Slide copy kicker="A sampler">

**Koala Sampler** is the app this class makes music in. A **sampler** grabs a piece of real sound — a voice, a clap, a bit of a song — and lets you play it back like an instrument. Just like a recipe needs its ingredients, a beat needs its sounds first.

</Slide>
    <Slide kicker="SAMPLE" sub="Sample tab, empty. Empty pads are red.">
      <figure>
        <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-empty.png" alt="Koala Sampler Sample tab with sixteen empty red pads and the instruction PRESS AND HOLD A RED PAD TO RECORD." />
      </figure>
    </Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="A sampler">

Three tabs live at the top: **SAMPLE**, **SEQUENCE**, **PERFORM**. Today we only look at SAMPLE — the place sounds come from.

</Slide>
  </Section>

  <Section name="Pads">
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads and the red instruction">

The grid of squares is sixteen **pads**. An empty pad is red. A pad with a sound shows a little wave and a name.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads and the red instruction">

The whole first skill is written right on the empty screen: **PRESS AND HOLD A RED PAD TO RECORD.** Hold to catch a sound. Let go to stop.

</Slide>
    <Slide kicker="RECORDING" sub="Hold a pad. It says RECORDING and counts.">
      <figure>
        <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-recording.png" alt="Koala Sample tab while recording. Pad 2 is solid pink and labeled RECORDING 00:03. Pad 1 already holds a sample." />
      </figure>
    </Slide>
    <Slide kicker="A pad with a sound" sub="A pad with a sound. The wave is the sound you caught.">
      <figure>
        <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-recorded.png" alt="Koala Sample tab after recording. The waveform editor is open. Knobs for VOL, PITCH, and PAN sit above the pad grid." />
      </figure>
    </Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads and the red instruction">

Tap a pad that has a sound and three knobs appear:

- **VOL** — how loud the pad is
- **PITCH** — how high or low it plays
- **PAN** — left, middle, or right

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads and the red instruction">

**DELETE** takes the sound off that pad.

</Slide>
  </Section>

  <Section name="Name your sound">
    <Slide copy kicker="Name your sound">

Under **TOOLS**, **LABEL** lets you give a pad a real name — *snap*, *hey*, *chair* — instead of leaving it called *Recording*. A sound you can name is a sound you can find again.

</Slide>

  <Section name="After this">
    <Slide copy kicker="What you should be able to do after this">

1. Point to SAMPLE, SEQUENCE, and PERFORM and say which one we are in.
2. Say what a sampler does in one sentence.
3. Say what **VOL**, **PITCH**, and **PAN** each change.
4. Say why naming a sound matters.

</Slide>

  <Section name="Catch one sound">
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

Koala day. How this runs depends on your class.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

**Ready 5th grade — iPad rotation.**

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

1. *I do:* Mr. Erreyon records a short sound onto a pad on the projector, then labels it.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

2. *We do:* The class names the three tabs and watches the pad turn from red to a wave.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

3. *You do:* A few students at a time open the **premade** project, record **one** short sound onto a pad, and **LABEL** it. Everyone else works the keyboard station or the content station. Rotate so each group gets a turn.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

**3rd / 4th grade (and any 5th not on iPads) — keyboards.**

- Watch the projected demo. Then at the **keyboard station**, find one note and play a short, simple sound pattern. Say out loud what makes the sound — the key, the finger, the push. That is *your* sample source for the day.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

**If the keyboard block will not hold:** split the room — half at keyboards, half at the **content station** (a short Rubin excerpt or article about where sounds come from + one notebook line). Swap halfway.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

**If instrument time breaks down:** whole class goes to the **content/literacy station**. Read the excerpt and write two sentences: name a sound in your life you would want to sample, and say why. No keyboards, no iPads.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Catch one sound">

Share: one or two volunteers name the sound they caught (or would catch).

</Slide>
