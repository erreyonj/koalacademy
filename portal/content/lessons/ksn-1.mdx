## Koala is where sound becomes a pad

**Koala Sampler** is the app this course makes music in. A sampler captures a piece of sound — a voice, a tap, a bar from a record — and lets you play it like an instrument. Fluency here is not extra. Every later unit assumes you can get a sound onto a pad without help.

You each have an iPad. Class samples for assigned work will be provided. We will talk about services like **Splice**; you do not need a Splice login for this course.

## Pads, banks, and the red instruction

The 4×4 grid is sixteen pads. Empty pads are red. A loaded pad shows a miniature waveform and a name.

Along the bottom, **A B C D** are banks — four sets of sixteen pads. We stay on **A** today. Bottom right, **^ SAMPLES** pulls up the library.

The sentence on the empty screen is the whole first skill: **PRESS AND HOLD A RED PAD TO RECORD.**

Headphones, **MIC**, and **FX** sit above the pads. The dotted meter is input level. If nothing moves when you make a sound, the mic is not hearing you.

Student keyboards in this room have no MIDI out, so they cannot drive Koala. Pads are the class default. The teacher keyboard *can* send MIDI; if you need that station, you schedule time. The in-app Keyboard view arrives in `KSN.2`.

## Hold to record, then look at what you caught

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-recording.png" alt="Koala Sample tab while recording. Pad 2 is solid pink and labeled RECORDING 00:03. Pad 1 already holds a sample." />
  <figcaption>Hold. The pad says RECORDING and counts.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. *I do:* Mr. Erreyon holds an empty pad, records a short sound, lets go.
2. *We do:* The room watches the pad turn from red to a waveform. Someone names what changed.
3. *You do:* You record **one** sound onto pad 1 — voice, body percussion, or a found sound in reach. Short is better than long.

Let go to stop. If you keep holding, you keep recording. Delete is a button, not a crisis.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-recorded.png" alt="Koala Sample tab after recording. The waveform editor is open. Knobs for VOL, PITCH, and PAN sit above the pad grid. EDIT is highlighted." />
  <figcaption>A loaded pad. Waveform up top; VOL, PITCH, PAN in the middle.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap the pad you just filled. The top window is that sample. **EDIT** (highlighted) shows the waveform. **MIX** is the other vertical tab — later. **DELETE** removes the sample from the pad, not from the iPad forever.

Three knobs:

- **VOL** — how loud this pad is
- **PITCH** — how high or low it plays
- **PAN** — left / center / right

**CHOKE** stops one pad when another plays. Leave it **OFF** today. **STRETCH** is time-stretch; do not need it for a one-shot you just recorded. Left and right arrows nudge the start and end of the waveform.

## TOOLS is a menu, not today's assignment

Open **TOOLS** so you know the list exists:

**LABEL** · **COLOR** · **CROP** · **NORMALIZE** · **TRIM SILENCE** · **AUTO-CHOP** · **SPLIT STEMS** · **MAKE MONO** · **BOUNCE** · **EXPORT**

Crop, chop, stems, bounce, and export will come back when a later lesson needs them. For this period, **LABEL** your pad so it is not still called Recording.

## Where samples live when they are not on a pad

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-samples-drawer.png" alt="Koala SAMPLES drawer listing BASICS, GET MORE SAMPLES, SONGS, MORE, and Samples, with ADD LOCATION and EDIT at the bottom." />
  <figcaption>^ SAMPLES. Class files live here; you do not buy packs for homework.</figcaption>
</figure>

Pull **^ SAMPLES**.

- **BASICS** — starter sounds that ship with the app
- **GET MORE SAMPLES** — extra packs. Not required.
- **SONGS** — Koala song files
- **MORE** / **Samples** — other folders, including anything we add for class
- **ADD LOCATION** — teacher-side. Cloud icon is the same idea.

If a lesson needs a specific file, it will already be on the iPad. Splice is a conversation about how producers find sounds in the world. It is not a login for this room.

## What you should be able to do after this

1. Point to SAMPLE, SEQUENCE, and PERFORM and say which one we are in.
2. Record a sound by pressing and holding an empty pad, then let go.
3. Set **VOL** so the pad is audible and not slamming.
4. **LABEL** the pad.
5. Open **^ SAMPLES** and find **BASICS**.
