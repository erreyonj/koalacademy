  <Section name="Koala">
    <Slide copy kicker="Koala">

**Koala Sampler** is the app this course makes music in. A sampler captures a piece of sound — a voice, a tap, a bar from a record — and lets you play it like an instrument. Every later unit assumes you can get a sound onto a pad without help.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Koala">

You each have an iPad. Class samples for assigned work will be provided. We will talk about services like **Splice**; you do not need a Splice login for this course.

</Slide>
    <Slide kicker="SAMPLE" sub="Sample tab, empty. Hold a red pad to record.">
      <figure>
        <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-empty.png" alt="Koala Sampler Sample tab with sixteen empty red pads and the instruction PRESS AND HOLD A RED PAD TO RECORD." />
      </figure>
    </Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Koala">

Three tabs live at the top: **SAMPLE**, **SEQUENCE**, **PERFORM**. Today is only SAMPLE. Sequence and Perform wait until you have sounds worth arranging.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Koala">

On the right, the hamburger menu holds file and settings work — that is `KSN.5`, not today.

</Slide>

  <Section name="Pads">
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads, banks, and the red instruction">

The 4×4 grid is sixteen pads. Empty pads are red. A loaded pad shows a miniature waveform and a name.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads, banks, and the red instruction">

Along the bottom, **A B C D** are banks — four sets of sixteen pads. Bottom right, **^ SAMPLES** pulls up the library.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads, banks, and the red instruction">

The sentence on the empty screen is the whole first skill: **PRESS AND HOLD A RED PAD TO RECORD.**

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads, banks, and the red instruction">

Headphones, **MIC**, and **FX** sit above the pads. The dotted meter is input level. If nothing moves when you make a sound, the mic is not hearing you.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Pads, banks, and the red instruction">

Student keyboards in this room have no MIDI out, so they cannot drive Koala. Pads are the class default. The teacher keyboard *can* send MIDI; if you need that station, you schedule time. The in-app Keyboard view arrives in `KSN.2`.

</Slide>
  </Section>

  <Section name="Hold to record">
    <Slide kicker="RECORDING" sub="Hold. The pad says RECORDING and counts.">
      <figure>
        <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-recording.png" alt="Koala Sample tab while recording. Pad 2 is solid pink and labeled RECORDING 00:03. Pad 1 already holds a sample." />
      </figure>
    </Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Hold to record">

1. *I do:* Mr. Erreyon holds an empty pad, records a short sound, lets go.
2. *We do:* The room watches the pad turn from red to a waveform. Someone names what changed.
3. *You do:* You record **one** sound onto pad 1 — voice, body percussion, or a found sound in reach. Short is better than long.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Hold to record">

Let go to stop. If you keep holding, you keep recording.

</Slide>
    <Slide kicker="A loaded pad" sub="A loaded pad. Waveform up top; VOL, PITCH, PAN in the middle.">
      <figure>
        <img src="/assets/ksn-1/koala-sample-tab-recorded.png" alt="Koala Sample tab after recording. The waveform editor is open. Knobs for VOL, PITCH, and PAN sit above the pad grid. EDIT is highlighted." />
      </figure>
    </Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Hold to record">

Tap the pad you just filled. The top window is that sample. **EDIT** (highlighted) shows the waveform. **MIX** is the other vertical tab on the right side of the EDIT window. **DELETE** removes the sample from the pad.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Hold to record">

Three knobs:

- **VOL** — how loud this pad is
- **PITCH** — how high or low it plays
- **PAN** — left / center / right

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="Hold to record">

**CHOKE** stops one pad when another plays. **STRETCH** is time-stretch: you would more likely use this to work with a preexisting song so that you can make sure the section you are sampling fits into your composition. Left and right arrows nudge the start and end of the waveform.

</Slide>
  </Section>

  <Section name="After this">
    <Slide copy kicker="What you should be able to do after this">

1. Point to SAMPLE, SEQUENCE, and PERFORM and say which one we are in.
2. Record a sound by pressing and holding an empty pad, then let go.
3. Set **VOL** so the pad is audible and not slamming.
4. **LABEL** the pad.
5. Open **^ SAMPLES** and find **BASICS**.

</Slide>
  </Section>
