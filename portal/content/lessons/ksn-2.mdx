## Sequence is where pads become a pattern

Last period you got sound onto a pad. Today those pads become a pattern you can play back without tapping every hit yourself.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-empty.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab empty. SEQUENCE is highlighted. Play, record, and 110 BPM sit above eight empty sequence slots and a 4x4 grid of empty pads." />
  <figcaption>Sequence tab, empty. Pads wait below; sequence slots sit above them.</figcaption>
</figure>

Three tabs at the top: **SAMPLE**, **SEQUENCE**, **PERFORM**. Today is only SEQUENCE. Sample is where the sounds live. Perform is effects — later.

Play (triangle), Record (red circle), the BPM number, and the metronome sit under the tabs. Empty sequence slots are the eight rectangles in the middle. Left and right arrows page through more slots. **CLEAR** wipes the pattern you are on. **1 BAR** (or **4 BARS**) is how long that pattern lasts.

**MUTE** and **SOLO** live bottom left. Leave them. The hamburger is still `KSN.5`.

You need sounds on pads before a sequence is worth recording. Use the pad you named in `KSN.1`, or pull something from **BASICS**. Empty pads give you a silent pattern.

## Sequences on the machine

A **sequence** is one looping section — a bar or a few bars of hits. You can keep several of them and switch later. Today you make **one**.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-sequences-loaded.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab with several sequence slots filled with light-blue waveform thumbnails. The 4x4 pad grid below shows loaded samples." />
  <figcaption>Filled sequence slots. A thumbnail means that slot already has a pattern.</figcaption>
</figure>

Tap an empty slot to work on a new pattern. Tap a filled one to hear what is already there.

The pads under the slots are the same pads as Sample tab — bank **A B C D**, **^ SAMPLES** still bottom right. Sequencing does not copy the sound; it plays the pad.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-samples.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab with empty sequence slots above a 4x4 grid of loaded blue pads showing waveforms and truncated sample names. Length is set to 4 BARS." />
  <figcaption>Pads in Sequence. Length here is 4 BARS — long enough for a phrase, short enough to finish in a period.</figcaption>
</figure>

**UNDO** / **REDO** sit bottom left once you have started recording. Wrong hit is not a crisis. Undo it.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-undo-redo.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab with UNDO highlighted in cyan and REDO beside it on the bottom left. Sequence slots and loaded pads fill the rest of the screen." />
  <figcaption>UNDO / REDO. Use them before you reach for CLEAR.</figcaption>
</figure>

To throw a sequence away, drag its slot onto the trash.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-delete-sequence.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab while deleting. A sequence thumbnail is being dragged toward a trash can icon above the sequence slots." />
  <figcaption>Drag the slot to the trash. That deletes the pattern, not the pads.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Map modes: Grid, Keyboard, Note Repeat

The control left of **ARP** is the map-mode picker. Tap it. Today you need three of the names on that list.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-map-modes.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab with the map-mode control labeled CHOOSE highlighted above the pad grid." />
  <figcaption>CHOOSE. That is how you pick Grid, Keyboard, or Note Repeat.</figcaption>
</figure>

**GRID** is the drum-machine view. Steps across the bar; rows are pads or pitches. Tap a cell to put a hit there. Tap again to take it off. This is the default for beats.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-map-mode-grid.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab in Grid map mode. A dropdown lists GRID, KEYBOARD, NOTE REPEAT, and VELOCITY, with GRID selected. A step grid below shows several filled cells." />
  <figcaption>GRID. Filled cells are hits. Empty cells are rest.</figcaption>
</figure>

**KEYBOARD** is the in-app piano. Student keyboards in this room still have no MIDI out — pads remain the class default — but if the sound wants pitches, this is the view. Bottom says **CHROMATIC** (or another scale). **C1** / **C2** are octaves.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-map-mode-keyboard.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab in Keyboard map mode. A two-octave on-screen piano shows C1 and C2. CHROMATIC is selected at the bottom." />
  <figcaption>KEYBOARD. Play pitches on screen. MIDI into Koala is still a teacher-station workflow.</figcaption>
</figure>

**NOTE REPEAT** holds a subdivision under your finger: **4**, **8**, **16**, **32**, **64**. Hold, and the pad re-triggers on that grid. Hats, shakers, rolls — anything that needs to stutter in time.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-map-mode-note-repeat.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab in Note Repeat map mode. Five columns labeled 4, 8, 16, 32, and 64 fill the lower half of the screen." />
  <figcaption>NOTE REPEAT. Hold 16 and the pad chatters sixteenths until you let go.</figcaption>
</figure>

Listen from 0:11. The repeating percussion is the feel this mode is for — not a copy of the record, a picture of the idea.

## Transport: play, record, tempo, quantize

Tap the BPM number. That panel is tempo, meter, quantize, and how sequences hand off.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-quantize-tempo-controls.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab with the tempo panel open. 113 BPM, TAP, plus and minus, BEATS PER BAR set to 4, QUANTIZE OFF, SEQ START/END, and SWING." />
  <figcaption>Tempo panel. TAP if you have a pulse; + / − if you want a number.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Play** — hear the sequence
- **Record** — red circle. Hits you play land in the pattern
- **BPM** — how fast. **TAP** to match a pulse; **+** / **−** to nudge
- **BEATS PER BAR** — leave at **4** today
- **QUANTIZE** — pulls hits onto the grid. **OFF** keeps your timing; turn it on if the pattern feels drunk and you wanted it tight
- **SWING** — later

Adding a note: record, or tap a cell in Grid. Deleting: tap the cell off, or **UNDO**. Length of the whole pattern is **1 BAR** / **4 BARS** above the pads, not the same thing as how long one note lasts. Note length and velocity in detail are `KSN.4`.

## Queue, save, and the settings exit

When you have more than one sequence, Koala has to know **when** the next one starts. That is the queue.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-2/koala-sequence-tab-sequence-queue-options.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab with the queue dropdown open. Options listed are OFF, 1 BEAT, 1 BAR, and SEQ END, which is highlighted. AUTO-PLAY is unchecked." />
  <figcaption>Queue. Class default is SEQ END — the new section waits until this one finishes.</figcaption>
</figure>

**SEQ END** means the pattern you are in finishes the phrase before the next one takes over. You do not have to tap the next section on beat. That is the setting this room keeps.

Saving the pattern is saving the **song** — the sequences live inside it, not as a separate “export this loop” file for today. Bouncing the sequence out as audio is the same family of job as **BOUNCE** / **EXPORT** on the Sample tab: named so you know it exists, not assigned. File work is `KSN.5`.

Exit ticket is smaller than a bounce. Export the **settings** file into the class folder so the next time you open Koala, queue is already **SEQ END**.

## What you should be able to do after this

1. Point to SEQUENCE and say how it differs from SAMPLE.
2. Record **one** pattern onto a sequence slot from pads you already have.
3. Switch map mode between **GRID**, **KEYBOARD**, and **NOTE REPEAT**, and say what each is for.
4. Set tempo and turn **QUANTIZE** on or off on purpose.
5. Set sequence queue to **SEQ END** and export settings to the class folder.
