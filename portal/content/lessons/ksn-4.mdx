## Step sequencer is the zoom-in

In `KSN.2` you recorded a pattern in **GRID** — tap cells, hit Record, hear it loop. That is enough to make a beat. Today you open the **step sequencer** — the editor where one hit at a time gets length, loudness, and even a coin flip.

There are more buttons here than on the Sequence tab. That is normal. **Persistence** means you do what you already know first, then come back to what is hard — not quitting because the screen looks crowded.

You still need sounds on pads and at least one sequence worth editing. Empty pads give you an empty grid.

## How you get in

Stay on **SEQUENCE**. Pick the slot you want to fix. Two ways into the step sequencer:

- **Double-tap** any sequence block (the rectangle with the waveform thumbnail).
- Or tap the **piano keyboard** icon above the pad grid.

Either opens the step sequencer full screen.

Each sequence block **defaults to 1 BAR**. Tap the bar-length button on the Sequence tab to change it — **+** / **−** adds or removes bars. **DOUBLE UP** copies what you have and doubles the length — useful when a 4-bar loop needs to become 8.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-sequence-tab-bar-select.PNG" alt="Koala Sequence tab with the bar-length menu open. Options include DOUBLE UP and 8 BARS with plus and minus controls." />
  <figcaption>Bar length button on Sequence tab. Default is 1 BAR; this screenshot shows 8. Set length here or inside the editor.</figcaption>
</figure>

Close the editor with **X** top right when you are done. **Save** the song when the period ends — same habit as `KSN.5`.

## Layout: timeline, rows, tools

The main view is a **grid**. Down the left: pad names **A1–A16** (bank **A**). Across the top: **bars** and beats. Each row is one pad; each block is one hit.

Bottom toolbar, left to right:

- **Pencil** — draw and erase hits
- **VEL** — velocity (how hard the hit fires)
- **Dice** — probability / chance
- **SNAP** — grid size (and **SNAP END** for how note tails land)
- **UNDO** / **REDO**

## Drum machine view and piano roll

What you just saw is the **drum machine** view — one row per pad, best for beats and one-shots.

Tap the **keyboard** icon in the top bar to switch to **piano roll**. The left edge becomes **pitches** (**C4**, **C3**, **C2** …) instead of pad names. Same pencil and tools; the rows mean notes, not just drums.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-piano-roll-view.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer in piano roll view. A vertical keyboard on the left shows C4, C3, and C2. The grid is empty with SNAP set to 1/16." />
  <figcaption>Piano roll. Rows are pitches. Use this when the sound needs a melody, not just a hit.</figcaption>
</figure>

Class default for today: stay in **drum machine** view unless Mr. Erreyon sends you to piano roll on purpose.

## Pencil: draw, length, select

**Pencil** selected means tap or drag on the grid to place a hit. Tap again to remove it. A hit is a **horizontal bar** — short bar is a staccato tap; long bar holds the sound.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-looping-2.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer with pencil active. A red horizontal bar on row A1 spans from beat 1.1 to 1.3 inside a one-bar loop." />
  <figcaption>One hit drawn on A1. The bar length is how long that pad stays triggered.</figcaption>
</figure>

The blue highlight across the top is the **loop region** — what plays when you press Play.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-looping.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer showing a highlighted loop region between beats 1.2 and 1.3 on a one-bar grid." />
  <figcaption>Loop region. Play repeats what is inside the highlight.</figcaption>
</figure>

Drag a **marquee** (dashed box) to select several hits at once — move them, delete them, or copy as a group.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-drag-selection.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer with a dashed rectangular selection box spanning rows A5 and A6 across part of the grid." />
  <figcaption>Marquee selection. Grab a whole phrase, not one cell at a time.</figcaption>
</figure>

You can still record from pads with the red **Record** button — pencil is for fixing what recording missed.

## Snap and quantize

**SNAP: 1/16** is the default grid — sixteenth-note steps. Hits land on the grid lines when you draw.

Open **SNAP** to change it. **FREE** turns the grid off — hits can sit between the lines. Use FREE on purpose, not because you gave up on the grid.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-free-snap.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer with SNAP set to FREE. A long red bar sits on row A1 with finer timeline divisions visible." />
  <figcaption>SNAP: FREE. Off the grid — only when you mean it.</figcaption>
</figure>

**SNAP END** (inside the SNAP menu) is how note **endings** get pulled onto the grid — separate from where the hit **starts**. Same family of idea as **QUANTIZE** on the Sequence tab in `KSN.2`, but here you are editing one note at a time.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-quantize.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer with the SNAP END menu open. Options include 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 highlighted, 1/32, and FREE." />
  <figcaption>SNAP END. Where the tail of the note snaps — 1/16 is a solid default.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Velocity: loud and quiet in the same pattern

Every hit has **velocity** — how hard it triggers. Same pad, same step, different velocity → the loop breathes instead of machine-gunning.

Tap **VEL**. Tap or drag a hit. Koala shows **VELOCITY: 67%** (or whatever you set). Higher = louder; lower = ghost notes and accents.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-velocity-tool.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer with VEL active. A pink hit on row A7 shows a tooltip reading VELOCITY 67 percent." />
  <figcaption>VEL tool. One hit, one number — change it, play, listen.</figcaption>
</figure>

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-velocity-adjust.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer with VEL highlighted in cyan and a single red hit at the start of row A7." />
  <figcaption>Velocity mode on. Adjust one pad before you adjust all of them.</figcaption>
</figure>

Map-mode **VELOCITY** from `KSN.2` is the performance view — hold a pad, slide up and down. **VEL** in the step editor is the surgery view — exact hits, exact numbers.

## Probability: chance and variation

Tap the **dice**. Tap a hit. **Chance: 40%** means that hit plays about two loops out of five — not every time. Same pattern, slightly different each pass.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/ksn-4/koala-step-seq-main-view-chance-tool.PNG" alt="Koala step sequencer with the dice tool active. A long red hit on row A7 shows Chance 40 percent." />
  <figcaption>Chance. 100% = always; 0% = never. Start with one ghost hit, not the whole row.</figcaption>
</figure>

This is optional today. One hit with chance below 100% is enough to prove you found the tool. The rest of the row can stay at 100%.

## One tool at a time

You do not need every button this period. Order that works:

1. Open the editor on a pattern you already have (`KSN.2`).
2. **Pencil** — fix one wrong hit or add one missing hit.
3. **VEL** — change one velocity; play back.
4. **Dice** — only if steps 1–3 are done and you still have time.

If the screen overwhelms you, close with **X**, play the pattern on the Sequence tab, and open the editor again. That is persistence — not magic.

## What you should be able to do after this

1. Open the step sequencer from **SEQUENCE** and say what the rows and columns mean.
2. Switch between **drum machine** and **piano roll** view and say when each is for.
3. Draw or erase at least one hit with **Pencil**.
4. Change **velocity** on at least one hit and hear the difference.
5. Name **VEL**, **dice**, and **SNAP** — even if you only used one of them today.
