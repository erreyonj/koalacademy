<Notes>

Movement today is Sky and Ground on guitar: skinny strings for high, fat strings for low. Skip the two rules slides once the class knows the game. The Listen slides are the harder round with real music.

<Activate title="Sky and Ground">

Students stand in their spots — no walking circle.

1. *I do:* Teacher plays a low sound and gets small near the ground. Then a high sound and stretches to the sky. Name it: high. Low.
2. *We do:* Class matches in place while the teacher still models the body.
3. *You do:* Teacher stops modeling. Students show high or low on their own. Watch who looks at neighbors.

The class wins the round when the stretch and the shrink happen together, on the sound.

Full rules live in the game bank: Sky and Ground.

</Activate>

On the Listen slides: the first clip is **low**. The second is **high** and starts at 2:50. The last is a trick with **both**. If the class copies neighbors instead of listening, that is the diagnostic.

</Notes>
