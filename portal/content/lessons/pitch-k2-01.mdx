## High is up. Low is down.

A high sound lives up high — like a bird, a whistle, or a singer way up there. A low sound lives down low — like a truck, a rumble, or a bass.

Today we only care about those two. If you can show it with your body, you can hear it.

Mr. E will play some videos. Stretch tall for high. Get small for low. If the class copies neighbors instead of listening, that is the diagnostic.

## The sound moves. You move with it.

If the sound slides up, you grow. If it slides down, you shrink. You do not wait to see what your friend does.

First clip is **low**. Next is **high** — start at 2:50. Last one is a trick: it has **both**.

<Activate title="Sky and Ground">

Students stand in their spots — no walking circle this time. Mr. E plays the videos instead of guitar today.

1. *I do:* Teacher plays the low clip and gets small near the ground. Then the high clip and stretches to the sky. Name it: high. Low.
2. *We do:* Class matches in place while the teacher still models the body.
3. *You do:* The clips play. Students show high or low. The last clip is the trick — both. Watch who looks at neighbors.

The class wins the round when the stretch and the shrink happen together, on the sound.

Full rules live in the game bank: Sky and Ground.

</Activate>
