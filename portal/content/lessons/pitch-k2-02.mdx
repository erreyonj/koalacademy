<Notes>

Teach the five-finger slides before the game. Thumb on C, then D, E, F, G. Players use that spot as a warmup before a scale or a song. The last slide opens Keys.lite Warmups — pick Five Finger Positions to walk the drill.

Movement today is Bright Steps. The keyboard is the listen — white keys have to be visible.

<Activate title="Bright Steps">

Students stand in their spots. Three short rounds. About a minute of talk, then move.

**White keys**

1. *I do:* Teacher plays and names C up to the next C.
2. *We do:* Class sings the letters and steps once per note.
3. *You do:* Class steps while the teacher plays and stops modeling.

**Bright**

1. *I do:* Teacher names that walk a major scale and opens both arms.
2. *We do:* Class matches the open arms on the scale.
3. *You do:* Class shows bright only when they hear that scale. Play a short scramble that is not the scale and watch who still opens their arms.

**Tune and together**

1. *I do:* Teacher sings a three-note tune from those white keys, then plays a chord under it. Name melody (the tune) and harmony (the notes that go with the tune).
2. *We do:* Class sings the tune while the chord plays.
3. *You do:* On cue, point to the mouth for the tune, or open the hands for the notes that go with it.

K sings "up" and steps. Grade 2 says the letter names.

The class wins when the steps land with the notes, and the bright arms happen on the major scale.

Full rules live in the game bank: Bright Steps.
