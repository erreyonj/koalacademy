## Timbre is how it sounds

High and low are one thing. Loud and quiet are another. **Timbre** is the *feel* of the sound — smooth, scratchy, short, ringing.

A pencil tap and a jingle bell can both be quiet. They do not sound the same.

Today we only care about that feel. If you can name it, you can hear it.

## One word. Find it.

Mr. E gives the class **one** target word. You hunt. You test quiet. You bring back your best find.

The class decides if it matches. Surprising finds that still match get shine.
