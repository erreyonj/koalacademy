  <Section name="What sampling is">
    <Slide copy kicker="What sampling is">

**Sampling** means taking a piece of an existing recording and using it to make something new — a loop, a beat, a hook. Hip-hop turned this into an art form, and it is still everywhere today.

</Slide>
  </Section>

  <Section name="Looping the break">
    <Slide copy kicker="Before any app">

In the **1970s** in the Bronx, **DJ Kool Herc** played block parties. Instead of playing whole songs, he **looped the break** — the drum part everyone loved — so the crowd could dance longer. Two record players, one great part, over and over.

</Slide>

  <Section name="The machine">
    <Slide copy kicker="The machine that made it easy">

Later, a machine called the **MPC** let producers grab sounds and play them back with pads. Producer **J Dilla** became famous for chopping up records into brand-new beats.

</Slide>
    <Slide kicker="Listen" sub="The apps people use today do the same job — the idea has not changed.">

  <Section name="Give credit">
    <Slide copy kicker="Give credit">

Sampling runs into **ownership** fast. If you use someone's sound, the fair thing is to **know where it came from** and **say so**. That is **Integrity** — doing the right thing even when no one is looking.

</Slide>
