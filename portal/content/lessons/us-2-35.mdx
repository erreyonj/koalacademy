  <Section name="One sample">
    <Slide copy kicker="One sample can be the whole song">

Last time you learned what sampling is. Today, one song: **Aaliyah** — *We Need a Resolution* (2001), made by producer **Timbaland**.

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="One sample can be the whole song">

Most beats have lots of parts. This one is built **on one sample** — a small piece of sound, looped and changed until it carries the whole mood. Timbaland was famous for picking **strange** sounds most people would skip. That takes **Risk** — trying something new even when it is hard. Your elevator question was the same idea.

</Slide>

  <Section name="Where the sample came from">
    <Slide copy kicker="Where the sample came from">

The sample came from a **movie score** — not a typical music record. Timbaland found it in a place other producers were not looking, changed it, and built a hit on top.

</Slide>
