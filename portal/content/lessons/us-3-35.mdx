## One voice, changed

Producers can take a **singer's voice** from an old record and change it. **Kanye West** did this in *Through the Wire* (2003). He took a piece of **Chaka Khan** singing and **pitched it up** so it sounds fast and bright — almost like a cartoon — then built his song on it.

## The original: Chaka Khan

First, the original. Listen for the big **chorus voice** that climbs.

That singing is the part Kanye borrowed — not the drums, the **voice**.

## The new song: pitched up

Now hear it sped up and higher in Kanye's song.

<YouTubeEmbed
  id="FGYvHk9LCY0"
  title="Through the Wire"
  artist="Kanye West"
/>

Same voice, new feeling. That is the lesson: **pitch changes the mood.** You are not just borrowing notes — you are bending how they feel.

There is a **Persistence** story here too. Kanye recorded this after a car accident, with his jaw wired shut — he kept going when it was hard. That is what your Do Now was about.
