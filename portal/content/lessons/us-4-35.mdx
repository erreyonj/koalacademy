  <Section name="The chain">
    <Slide copy kicker="A song can pass through many hands">

Sometimes a song is made by one person, then **covered** (sung again) by someone else, then **sampled** by a producer. That makes a **chain**. Today's job is to follow the chain and ask: **who should get credit?**

</Slide>
    <Slide copy kicker="A song can pass through many hands">

The song is *Lord I Need You*.

</Slide>
  </Section>

  <Section name="Give credit">
    <Slide copy kicker="Give credit down the whole chain">

When a song passes through many hands, **each person** helped make it. Fair credit means naming **everyone** in the chain — the writer, the singer, and the producer. Skipping one does not erase what they did. That is **Integrity**.

</Slide>
