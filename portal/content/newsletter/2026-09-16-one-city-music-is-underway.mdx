Every class in every grade has had at least one music meeting. So far we have talked about **basic rhythms** in K–2, some of the classes’ favorite music in 3rd and 4th, and **major and minor scales** (and how they sound) in 5–8.

## Notebooks, iPads, and homework

All music students should have a **music notebook** that can stay in class. I have requested extras for any student who is not able to get one. Do Nows, lesson and discussion notes, and more live in this notebook — it is also one of the ways the participation portion of the grade is tracked.

Where iPad work is core (6–8, with 5th grade under review), students know that class behavior overall and individual behavior affect iPad permissions. Students who lose iPad permissions will have separate assignments on days when iPad work is primary.

The first text we will examine in the higher grades is *The Creative Act* by Rick Rubin. Homework in general will be articles to review and write about in journals, then bring back to class.
