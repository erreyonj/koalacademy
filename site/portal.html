What it is today

  • A banded lesson library — intro lessons plus strand and production content as it ships.
  • Teacher-facing slides for classroom projection and rehearsal of CREW / CRISP culture.
  • The home for Vanguard listening embeds and activity blocks that match the playbook lesson shape.

What it is building toward

  • Coursework — sequenced assignments that sit beside the lesson library, not only static slides.
  • Homework — take-home tasks with a clear place to submit and return.
  • Metrics tracker — growth and completion visible to teachers, with student and parent individual login.
  • Interactive checks — in-portal questions (for example, finish this rhythm or melody) that unlock new material in the Resources section when answered.
