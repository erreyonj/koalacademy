Vanguard Songs · Grades 2 + 3

The year writing starts.

Second grade answers in single words. Third grade answers in sentences, with an optional second anchor to widen the year.

Status: scaffold. Anchors are still TBD. The players below hold demo tracks so the format can be reviewed — they are not the chosen anchors. Songs lock before the year starts and do not change mid-year.

These two grades are where listening becomes an artifact. Second graders write one word; third graders get the student guide, a sentence frame, and their first journal lines about a record they already know by heart.

Anchor — TBD

Grade 2 · one anchor · locked before day one

Second grade hears the downbeat inside this record — the same pulse Silent Ball asks them to pass on — and sorts its sounds by quality on the timbre chart.

  • Fall. Find the downbeat; clap only on beat one.
  • Winter. Chart three sounds from the recording by quality.
  • Spring. Written single-word responses: one word for the feeling, one for the sound.

Anchor one — TBD

Grade 3 · primary anchor · locked before day one

Third grade's primary anchor carries the first “why” questions: major or minor, and what in the music tells you so.

  • Fall. Predict the next section out loud before it arrives.
  • Winter. Major vs. minor mood by ear, with the recording as evidence.
  • Spring. Journal line with a sentence frame: “Today I noticed ______ in the music.”
