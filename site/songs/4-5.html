Koalacademy

Vanguard Songs · Grades 4 + 5

A weekly lens on the same record.

Fourth and fifth grade carry one to two anchors each, and every week points a different lens at them — form, drums, timbre, and finally what a producer would take.

Status: scaffold. Anchors are still TBD. The players below hold demo tracks so the format can be reviewed — they are not the chosen anchors. Songs lock before the year starts and do not change mid-year.

This is the band where the anchors stop being listening exercises and start being reference recordings. Fifth grade in particular pre-loads middle school: drum roles by ear, full-song form on a real record, and the first conversation about what sampling actually is — no devices, just ears.
