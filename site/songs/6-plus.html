Vanguard Songs · Grades 6, 7, 8

Three records. Every concept points back.

Each middle-grade class carries three anchor recordings for the year and keeps a running journal on them. Form, rhythm, harmony, production, and sampling all get tested against the same three songs.

Status: scaffold. Eight of the nine anchors are TBD; the grade 8 primary is a candidate, not a lock. Every player below is a placeholder until the lists close.

Selection for 6–8 adds one more test on top of the K–5 criteria: is there a usable excerpt for Koala work, and what would the rights conversation around it look like? An anchor that can't survive a sampling discussion is doing only half the job.

Grade 8 · the lens

Interpretive discourse: restraint vs. complexity, intent, meaning.

8·1

“Forever YHWH” — Jordan G. Welch (cover)

Grade 8 · candidate primary discourse piece · not yet locked

Player pending — candidate awaiting sign-off

Carried over from the original framework as a model of mature discourse: arrangement restraint, harmonic pacing, spiritual subtext, performance intention, and production minimalism.

  • Restraint. What was left out, and what does the space do?
  • Pacing. Where does the harmony move, and where does it refuse to?
  • Intent. What is this performance for, and how can you tell?

Questions that come back all year

  • What is the form? Where are the boundaries, and what marks them?
  • What is the rhythmic feel — straight or swung? Where do the drums sit?
  • What was left out, and what does the space do?
  • What would you sample from this record, and what would you owe the artist for it?

“What do you hear now that you didn't hear before?”
