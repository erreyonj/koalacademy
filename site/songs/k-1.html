Vanguard Songs · Grades K + 1

One song. All year.

Kindergarten and first grade each carry a single anchor recording from September to June. No handout at this age — the questions are spoken, modeled, and moved to.

Status: scaffold. Both anchors are still TBD. The players below hold demo tracks so the format can be reviewed — they are not the chosen anchors. Songs lock before the year starts and do not change mid-year.

For K and 1, an anchor earns its place with a clear beat, obvious section contrast, sounds a five-year-old can tell apart, and lyrics that hold up in a classroom. Sittings are three to five minutes; bodies and single words are the response format.

Kindergarten · one anchor · locked before day one

The kindergarten anchor does the work of a warm-up: the class walks the beat to it, freezes when it rests, and names one sound they can hear inside it.

  • Fall. Where is the beat? Can you keep it with your body?
  • Winter. What sounds do you hear? Can you point when one comes back?
  • Spring. How does this song feel — and what in the music makes it feel that way?

Grade 1 · one anchor · locked before day one

First grade keeps the beat without a spoken count and starts calling the A/B turn without a cue — the anchor is where Form Detective goes live.

  • Fall. Beat without counting; “what did you notice” as a spoken routine.
  • Winter. Two or three timbre words used correctly about this recording.
  • Spring. Signal the section change before the teacher does.

The question that bookends the year

“What do you hear now that you didn't hear before?”

— asked in the fall, and again in the spring
