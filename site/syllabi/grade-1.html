Grade 1 Music

Kindergarten play with the training wheels off: beat without a count, echoed rhythms, real words for sounds, and A/B turns nobody has to cue.

Status: scaffold. Sections marked TBD fill in after meeting cadence and calendar are confirmed. Program-wide context lives in the master syllabus.

Course description

First graders build on kindergarten play: they keep the beat without the teacher counting, echo short rhythms, describe sounds with a growing timbre vocabulary, and start turning on A/B section changes without cues. Written work remains coloring and tracing music symbols.

StrandEmphasis this year

PITCHMelodic direction (up/down) with hand movement; echo singing grows
TIMBRETwo or three descriptor words used correctly

Games rotation

Beat Freeze (one bar, count fades out), Timbre Scavenger (teams, justify the find), and Form Detective (uncued) — the K–1 and 2–3 variations.
