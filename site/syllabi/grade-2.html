Grade 2 Music

The beat moves inside: Silent Ball passes land on the downbeat, rhythms stretch to two bars, and written notation gets read for the first time.

Status: scaffold. Period length and the 6-day rotation are locked. Year-long pacing fills in against the school calendar. Program-wide context lives in the master syllabus.

Course description

Second graders internalize the beat: Silent Ball arrives (passes on the downbeat only), rhythms stretch to two bars with multiple rests, and the first real notation reading begins — matching heard rhythms to simple written ones. Written work moves from coloring to matching and one-measure fill-ins.

Games rotation

Silent Ball (downbeat, teacher counts the first round), Beat Freeze (two bars, multiple rests), Timbre Scavenger (charting), and Form Detective (A/B/C) — the 2–3 variations.

Assessment and grading

Observation plus one or two collected pages per quarter (matching, fill-ins) for progress and gradebook evidence, with targeted correctives when a gap shows up (2–3 written-work band).
