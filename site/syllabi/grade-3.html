Grade 3 Music

Playing with knowledge, not just movement: reading rhythms in 4/4, hearing major against minor, and answering in full sentences.

Status: scaffold. Sections marked TBD fill in after meeting cadence and calendar are confirmed. Program-wide context lives in the master syllabus.

Course description

Third graders start playing with knowledge, not just movement: Balloon War Trivia enters as review, listening responses become sentences, and reading simple rhythms in 4/4 becomes expected rather than introduced. An optional second Vanguard Song deepens the listening year.
