Course description

The bridge year. Fifth graders consolidate everything the strands built — internal 4/4 counting, subdivision, staff reading, full-song form, timbre vocabulary — so that 6th grade can walk into the Koalacademy production course without the theory front-load that used to open it. First exposure to what sampling is happens here, by ear, without devices.
