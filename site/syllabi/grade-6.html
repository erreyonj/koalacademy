Grade 6 Music

Entry into the Koalacademy production course — theory in service of making, with intentional listening as the year’s lens.

Status: scaffold. This syllabus is intentionally thin: grades 6–8 follow the existing Koalacademy course, and its lesson content lives in the course document, not here. Program-wide context lives in the master syllabus.

Course description

Sixth grade enters the Koalacademy production curriculum: music theory in service of making, sampling history, drum design, song form, sampling rights, and an original project sampled from songs the students choose. The 6th-grade lens on it all is introduction to intentional listening — emotional recognition and basic structure. See the Vanguard vertical alignment on the 6–8 Vanguard Songs page.

Year mapping

The Koalacademy course is 20 weeks; the school year is roughly 36. Extra weeks absorb the respaced theory spiral, Vanguard Song listening, games, and production-day logistics. The spine runs roughly four weeks per unit; exact calendar placement is set per section. Units follow the five-unit structure in the master syllabus; C1/C2 are the two components within each unit.

Devices

Thirty iPads are in the building, so production days run 1:1. Production vs. unplugged is a lesson choice, not a rental calendar. Student keyboards have no MIDI out; the teacher keyboard does, by appointment — see the playbook teaching notes.

Follows the Koalacademy project spine plus journals and exit tickets as graded work; theory worksheets lean corrective (6–8 written-work band).
