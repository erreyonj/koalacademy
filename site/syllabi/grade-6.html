Grade 6 Music

Entry into the Koalacademy production course — theory in service of making, with intentional listening as the year’s lens.

Status: scaffold. This syllabus is intentionally thin: grades 6–8 follow the existing Koalacademy course, and its lesson content lives in the course document, not here. Program-wide context lives in the master syllabus.

Course description

Sixth grade enters the Koalacademy production curriculum: music theory in service of making, sampling history, drum design, song form, sampling rights, and an original project sampled from songs the students choose. The 6th-grade lens on it all is introduction to intentional listening — emotional recognition and basic structure. See the Vanguard vertical alignment on the 6–8 Vanguard Songs page.

Year mapping

The existing course is 20 weeks; the school year is roughly 36. Extra weeks absorb the respaced theory spiral, Vanguard Song listening, games, and device-day logistics. Exact pacing TBD.

Devices

No 1:1 access assumed. Device-dependent components (KSN, D4, AW work weeks, AEP, PERFORMANCE) cluster onto reserved or rented device days, per the playbook teaching notes.

Vanguard Songs

Three anchors locked — Primary: “Let's Dream In The Moonlight” — Samara Joy [K-2 Companion]. Students receive the 6–8 student guide and keep a listening journal. See the 6–8 Vanguard Songs page.

Assessment and grading

Follows the Koalacademy project spine plus journals and exit tickets as graded work; theory worksheets lean corrective (6–8 written-work band). Gradebook mapping TBD.
