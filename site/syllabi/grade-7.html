Status: scaffold. This syllabus is intentionally thin: grades 6–8 follow the existing Koalacademy course, and its lesson content lives in the course document, not here. Program-wide context lives in the master syllabus.

Course description

Seventh grade continues the Koalacademy production curriculum with a sharper lens: deeper structural awareness, contrast, and stylistic identity. See the Vanguard vertical alignment on the 6 + Vanguard Songs page. Extended theory (BMT.2 modes, BMT.3 relative and parallel keys) likely lands here rather than in 6th, per the scope and sequence.
