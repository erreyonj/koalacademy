Kindergarten Music

Music through play: steady beat in the body, freezing on silence, hunting the room for sounds, and echoing short songs.

Status: scaffold. Period length and the 6-day rotation are locked. Year-long pacing fills in against the school calendar. Program-wide context lives in the master syllabus.

Course description

Music through play. Kindergartners keep a steady beat with their bodies, freeze on silences, hunt for sounds around the room, echo short songs, and learn that music has parts that repeat and change. Everything is movement, voice, and games — no written work beyond coloring music symbols.

Meeting cadence

About 27–28 minutes, twice in a typical week. Use the 30-minute assembly.

Games rotation

Beat Freeze (one bar, teacher counts aloud), Timbre Scavenger (one class-wide target word), and Form Detective (teacher cues changes) — the K–1 variations.
