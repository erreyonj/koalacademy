# Unit 1: Music Theory and Koala Sampler Navigation

Full lesson content: [Unit 1 in KOALACADEMY.md](../../curriculum/KOALACADEMY.md#unit-1-music-theory-and-koala-sampler-navigation)

## Unit goals

Give students the shared musical vocabulary the rest of the course depends on, and make them
fluent enough in Koala Sampler to get an idea from a sound source to a playable pattern without
help.

By the end of the unit a student can name notes, scales, modes, and key relationships, count in
4/4, 3/4, and 6/8, identify the four core drum sounds and their roles, and move confidently
through every tab in Koala Sampler.
