# Unit 2: Understanding Sampling and Digital Drumming / Drum Design

Full lesson content: [Unit 2 in KOALACADEMY.md](../../curriculum/KOALACADEMY.md#unit-2-understanding-sampling-and-digital-drumming--drum-design)

## Unit goals

Place sampling in its historical lineage and teach students to hear what a sample is doing in a
record, then apply the same analytical ear to drum programming and processing.

By the end of the unit a student can distinguish sampling from interpolation, analyze how a sample
functions inside a finished record, build a drum pattern with an intended groove, apply swing and
note repeat with purpose, and process drums without destroying them.
