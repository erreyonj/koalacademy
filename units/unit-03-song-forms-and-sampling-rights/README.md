# Unit 3: Song Forms in Popular Music and Sampling Rights

Full lesson content: [Unit 3 in KOALACADEMY.md](../../curriculum/KOALACADEMY.md#unit-3-song-forms-in-popular-music-and-sampling-rights)

## Unit goals

Teach students to map and design song structure, and to understand who owns what in a piece of
music before they start sampling for their own project.

By the end of the unit a student can diagram the form of any record, explain AABA and how modern
records depart from it, write a beat flip or major transition on purpose, describe the difference
between composition and publishing ownership, and lay out a realistic path to clearing a sample.
