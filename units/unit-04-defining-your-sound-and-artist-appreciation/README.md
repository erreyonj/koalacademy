# Unit 4: Defining Your Sound and Artist Appreciation

Full lesson content: [Unit 4 in KOALACADEMY.md](../../curriculum/KOALACADEMY.md#unit-4-defining-your-sound-and-artist-appreciation)

## Unit goals

Turn the course over to the students. They define what their own sound consists of, gather and
analyze the evidence for it, then commit to the three songs their final project will sample.

By the end of the unit a student has a written sound statement, a documented ten-song playlist,
two rounds of analysis on their selections, three locked final project sources with completed
rights sheets, and a working Koala project with a full arrangement.
